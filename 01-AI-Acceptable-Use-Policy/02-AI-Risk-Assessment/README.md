# AI Risk Assessment

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how NoorTech Solutions identifies, evaluates, and manages risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence systems.

Risk assessments are a critical component of AI governance because they help organizations identify potential security, privacy, legal, operational, and ethical risks before AI systems are deployed.

This project follows a structured risk management approach that supports responsible AI adoption while protecting customers, employees, and business operations.

---

## Objectives

- Identify AI-related risks
- Evaluate business impact
- Assess likelihood of occurrence
- Define mitigation strategies
- Support informed decision-making
