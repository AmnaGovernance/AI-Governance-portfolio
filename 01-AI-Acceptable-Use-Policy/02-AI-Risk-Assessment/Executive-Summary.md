# Executive Summary

Artificial intelligence offers significant opportunities to improve productivity, innovation, and decision-making. However, AI also introduces new risks that organizations must proactively manage.

This AI Risk Assessment was developed for NoorTech Solutions to identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks associated with the use of AI technologies across the organization.

The assessment examines cybersecurity, data privacy, regulatory compliance, operational reliability, ethical considerations, and human oversight. By identifying potential risks early, NoorTech Solutions can implement appropriate controls to reduce business impact while supporting responsible and trustworthy AI adoption.

This document complements the AI Acceptable Use Policy and forms part of NoorTech Solutions' broader AI Governance Framework.
