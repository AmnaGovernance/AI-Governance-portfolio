# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear expectations for the responsible, secure, and ethical use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools at NoorTech Solutions.

This policy helps employees use AI to improve productivity while protecting company information, customer data, intellectual property, and business operations.
