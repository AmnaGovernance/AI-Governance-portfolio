# Executive Summary
## Overview
Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming the way organizations work by improving productivity,
supporting decision-making, and helping employees complete tasks more efficiently. As AI tools become more common in the workplace, 
it is important to ensure they are used responsibly, securely, and ethically.
## Business Need
Employees across NoorTech Solutions are beginning to use AI tools to draft emails, summarize documents, generate ideas, and assist with daily tasks. 
While these tools offer many benefits, they also
introduce risks related to data privacy, cybersecurity, inaccurate information, intellectual property, and regulatory compliance.
Without clear guidance, employees may unintentionally expose confidential information, rely on inaccurate AI-generated content, or use AI in ways that conflict with company policies.
## Purpose
The AI Acceptable Use Policy establishes clear expectations for the safe and responsible use of AI across NoorTech Solutions. It provides employees with guidance
on appropriate AI use while protecting company information, customer data, and business operations.
## Expected Outcomes
By implementing this policy, NoorTech Solutions aims to:
- Promote responsible and ethical AI use
- Protect sensitive company and customer information
- Reduce cybersecurity and compliance risks
- Encourage innovation while maintaining human oversight
- Build employee awareness of AI-related risks and responsibilities
