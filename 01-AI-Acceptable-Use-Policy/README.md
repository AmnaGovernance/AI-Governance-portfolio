#AI Acceptable Use Policy
## Project Overview
This project contains the first AI governance policy developed for the fictional organization, NoorTech Solutions.
The purpose of this policy is to establish clear expectations for the responsible, secure, and ethical use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools across the organization.
This project demonstrates how AI governance can help organizations reduce risk while encouraging innovation.
## Objectives
- Promote responsible AI use
- Protect company and customer data
- Establish employee responsibilities
- Reduce security and privacy risks
- Support ethical and compliant AI adoption
