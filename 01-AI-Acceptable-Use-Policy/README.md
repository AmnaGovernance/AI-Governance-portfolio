# AI Acceptable Use Policy

## Project Overview

This project contains the first AI governance policy developed for the fictional organization, NoorTech Solutions. The purpose of this policy is to establish clear expectations for the responsible, secure, and ethical use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools across the organization.

This project demonstrates how AI governance can reduce organizational risk while enabling innovation and productivity.

---

## Objectives

- Promote responsible AI use
- Protect company and customer data
- Establish employee responsibilities
- Reduce security and privacy risks
- Support ethical and compliant AI adoption

---

## Key Features

- AI Acceptable Use Policy
- Executive Summary
- Employee Responsibilities
- Approved & Prohibited AI Uses
- Data Protection Requirements
- Human Oversight Principles
- Incident Reporting Guidance
